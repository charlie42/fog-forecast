How accurate the fog forecast is

The forecast was checked on 6,115 mornings from October to March since January 2024, at airports in 14 European cities. For each morning, the forecast made the day before was compared with the visibility the airport reported. Each month was tested with a formula that was fitted without that month.

The forecast works for fog that forms overnight in calm air. It does not work for fog that comes in from the sea, so coastal cities such as Los Angeles and San Francisco have no page.

Each forecast is for one weather station, usually the city's airport, and fog is local. The airport can be in fog while the city centre is clear, or the other way round. The station is written on each city's page.

Delhi has a formula of its own and was tested separately. There the forecast gives too high a chance: mornings given about 60% had fog 44% of the time.

The method and all test results are on GitHub.
