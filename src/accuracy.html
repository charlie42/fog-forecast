How accurate the fog forecast is

The forecast was checked on 6,115 mornings from October to March since January 2024, at airports in 14 European cities. For each morning, the forecast made the day before was compared with the visibility the airport reported. The formula had not seen the month it was tested on.

Fog came on 8% of all mornings. The mornings given 30% or more held half of all the fog.

The forecast works for fog that forms overnight in calm air. It does not work for fog that comes in from the sea, so coastal cities such as Los Angeles and San Francisco have no page. An airport is one point, often outside the city. Delhi has a formula of its own and was tested separately. Its numbers run high: mornings given about 60% had fog 44% of the time.
