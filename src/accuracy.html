How accurate the fog forecast is

The forecast was checked on 10,232 mornings since January 2024 in 23 cities in Europe, the United States and New Zealand, at the weather station named on each city's page. The mornings are from October to March, and from April to September in New Zealand. For each morning, the forecast made the day before was compared with what happened. A morning was counted as foggy when the visibility measured at the station was under 1 km for at least two hours between 4 and 10 h. Each winter was tested with a formula that was fitted without that winter.

The forecast works for fog that forms overnight in calm air. It does not work for fog that comes in from the sea, so coastal cities such as Los Angeles and San Francisco have no page.

Each forecast is for one weather station, usually the city's airport, and fog is local. The airport can be in fog while the city centre is clear, or the other way round. The station is written on each city's page.

Delhi, Lahore and Amritsar share a formula of their own and were tested separately. In Delhi the forecast gives too high a chance: mornings given about 60% had fog 52% of the time. In Lahore it is a little too high: mornings given about 80% had fog 74% of the time. In Amritsar it gives too low a chance: mornings given about 60% had fog 74% of the time.

The method and all test results are on GitHub.
